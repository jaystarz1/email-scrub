      Help: what to check
      

      What is caught automatically: names in headers, greetings and sign-offs; emails; phones; dates; addresses; labelled IDs, SINs and card numbers with a valid checksum, SSNs; links; confidentiality notices; valid IBANs; Canadian bank account numbers; IP and MAC addresses; valid VINs; GPS coordinates; social handles.
      

      What is flagged for you: yellow highlights mark possible names, ID-like numbers, file names, ages, profile links and ZIP codes. Organisation-specific numbers, such as employee or service numbers, are flagged rather than replaced because the tool cannot know every format.
      

      What is not reliably caught: single names in prose, nicknames, and context that identifies someone without naming them. Yellow highlights cover some of this. You are responsible for the final check. Scroll the result before you copy.
      

      People versus Always remove: Me becomes <ME>. This thread and Saved people use an optional label or <PERSON-n>. Me is matched first; this-thread names take precedence over the same saved name forms. Always remove handles project names, organisations or any other identifying terms, one per line.
      

      Reviewing the result: Replace hides every matching occurrence in this thread only. Always remove saves the item for future threads. Keep leaves the text unchanged and removes its yellow highlight for this thread. The review counter shows what remains; Next focuses the next Replace button and wraps at the end. Accept keeps the remaining yellow items and acknowledges any red leaks, then turns into Copy; a second click copies. Acceptance resets when the copy text changes or new flags or leaks appear, but Keep alone does not reset it. Red means a detail you asked to remove still appears. Review items lists the yellow items and confirmed leaks. Passing a leak check is not proof of anonymity.
      

      Preferences: only Me, Saved people, Always remove terms and highlighted exclusions are stored in this browser’s local storage and are not encrypted. Always remove saves an item; Replace and Keep apply to the current thread only and are not stored. This-thread people, threads, results and replacement maps are not saved. Saved people show only the date last used, updated when a name is actually replaced. Clear current thread removes its people, temporary exclusions and Keep list but keeps Me and Saved people. Save moves a row into Saved people. Search ignores case and accents. Forget is disabled during the sample; leave the sample first. Use Forget saved people and exclusions to erase preferences. If this computer is shared, use Forget when you're done.
      

      Check it yourself: open the browser’s developer tools, go to the Network tab, run a scrub, and confirm nothing is sent.
